# Welcome to Bootstrapp

A place to keep your notes, tasks, projects, and anything else you want to track. Files stay on your computer.

## Set up your workspace

Use **Project > Open Project** to pick a folder on your computer. That folder becomes your workspace, and Bootstrapp will remember it next time.

## What's already here

- **Notes** for writing things down, with tags and search.
- **Tasks** for checklists and kanban boards.
- **Practice** for coding exercises and step-through algorithms.
- **Pages** for building small websites and publishing them.
- **Extensions** browser to add more or remove what you don't use.

Treat this file as a scratchpad. Edit it, delete it, or replace it with whatever's useful to you.
